The problem

QA is fast. Manual defect handling isn't.

Old way
Inspect part, stop line, physically tag or carry defective item
Break workflow to log issues on clipboard or terminal
Managers have no live visibility — reports come hours later
TAQA way
Speak the defect — TAQA hears, identifies, and dispatches
Robotic arm picks up and removes the part automatically
Managers see every flagged part live on the web dashboard
